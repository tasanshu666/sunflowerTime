Hello, and thank you for the guidance. Below is the information requested under
Guideline 2.1. A screen recording captured on a physical device will be provided
in the Resolution Center reply.

QUICK START - PLEASE READ FIRST
- No account, login or password is required. The app is fully offline and opens
  directly on the child screen.
- PARENT AREA: tap "Parent Hub" at the TOP-RIGHT corner of the child screen. On
  the FIRST entry the app asks you to SET your own 4-digit PIN - there is no
  preset PIN, so please choose one, for example 1234. This gate exists so that a
  child cannot reach the parent area or the purchase entry.
- Child tabs: Today / Growth / Shop / Garden / Record. Tap a task to start a
  focus session.
- Parent tabs: My / Tasks / Rewards / Records / Settings. A reward claimed by
  the child appears here for approval.

1) PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
A focus timer for children roughly 4-12 years old, used together with a parent.
Problem it solves: children's study time is easily broken by distraction, and
parents lack a simple, non-punitive way to build focus habits and to see whether
the time was actually spent. Value it provides: focused minutes earn an in-app
currency ("sunlight"), and sunlight is spent growing plants in a small garden -
so effort turns into visible, patient progress instead of a score.

2) EXTERNAL SERVICES, TOOLS AND PLATFORMS
None are used to deliver core functionality. All data (tasks, rewards, focus
history, garden state) is stored in a local database on the device. There is no
server, no cloud sync, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no AI service and no
third-party data provider. The only external system involved is Apple's In-App
Purchase (StoreKit), used solely to process the single one-time purchase.

3) REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. Features and content are identical in every region. The interface is
localized in Simplified Chinese and English and follows the device language. The
only region-dependent element is the price of the in-app purchase, which the App
Store localizes automatically.

4) REGULATED INDUSTRY AND THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
The app is not in a regulated industry and does not provide any regulated
service. All illustrations, animations, audio and text were created for this app
by the developer; no third-party trademark, brand or copyrighted work is
included, so there is nothing to license.

5) IN-APP PURCHASE OVERVIEW
There is exactly one product: a non-consumable, "Tiny Sunflower Premium"
(com.sunflowertime.app.premium.lifetime), US$14.99 one-time, not a subscription.
It permanently unlocks: (a) one-click care for all plants in the garden - free
users can still care for each plant individually; (b) garden capacity from 4 pots
to 12 pots; (c) unlimited growth-task templates (free: up to 5); (d) unlimited
reward templates (free: up to 5); (e) up to 3 child profiles (free: 1); (f) title
perks that give plants small bonuses as the child's focus level rises.
Navigation: child screen -> "Parent Hub" (top-right) -> parent PIN -> "My" tab ->
gold card "Unlock full features" -> premium page -> "Unlock now" (price loaded
from the App Store) or "Restore purchases". The purchase entry is intentionally
placed inside the PIN-protected parent area so that a child cannot reach it.

6) PHYSICAL DEVICE TESTING
This build (1.0.0, build 2105) has been installed via TestFlight and exercised on
a physical iPhone running the latest iOS, and the screen recording is being
captured on that device. It begins with launching the app and shows the typical
user flow, including the in-app purchase flow.

No account-based features exist, therefore no demo account is needed and no
account deletion flow applies.

Hello, and thank you for the detailed guidance. Below is every item you asked
for. The same text has been added to the Notes field of App Review Information
for future reference.

1) SCREEN RECORDING
Attached. It was captured on a physical iPhone running the latest iOS, using the
submitted build (1.0.0, build 2105) installed via TestFlight. The recording
starts from launching the app and shows the typical user flow: first-run
consent, creating and selecting a task, running a focus session, the reward
screen, the garden and the shop, then the parent area and the in-app purchase
flow. The app has no account creation, login or account deletion, so those flows
do not exist in the app and are therefore not in the recording.

2) PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Tiny Sunflower is a focus timer for children roughly 4-12 years old, used
together with a parent. The problem it solves: children's study time is easily
broken by distractions, and parents lack a simple, non-punitive way to build
focus habits and to see whether the time was actually spent. The value: the
child starts a timer, focused minutes earn an in-app currency ("sunlight"), and
the sunlight is spent growing plants in a small garden - so effort turns into
visible, patient progress instead of a score. The parent side lets the parent
define tasks and rewards, approve what the child claims, review daily and weekly
reports, and set eye-care breaks. Everything runs fully offline: no accounts, no
ads, no tracking, no data leaves the device.

3) HOW TO SET UP AND ACCESS THE MAIN FEATURES
No account, login or credentials are required - the app works immediately after
install.
- Child area: opens on launch. Bottom tabs: Today, Growth, Shop, Garden,
  Record. Tap a task to start a focus session; the sun icon shows the balance.
- Parent area: tap the "Parent Hub" button in the TOP-RIGHT corner of the child
  screen. On the FIRST entry the app asks you to SET your own 4-digit PIN -
  there is no preset PIN, so please choose one, for example 1234. This gate
  exists so a child cannot reach the parent area or the purchase entry.
- Inside the parent area: My (entitlement, contact and website), Tasks, Rewards,
  Records/Reports, Settings (eye-care break, PIN change, delete data).
- Claim approval: when the child claims a reward, it appears for the parent to
  approve or reject.

4) EXTERNAL SERVICES, TOOLS AND PLATFORMS
None are used to deliver core functionality. All data (tasks, rewards, focus
history, garden state) is stored in a local database on the device; there is no
server, no cloud sync, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no AI service.
The only external system involved is Apple's In-App Purchase (StoreKit), used
solely to process the one-time purchase. The app uses a few open-source Flutter
plugins for local audio playback and for opening the system browser or mail app
(the support link and the support e-mail address); neither transmits app data.

5) REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
There are none. The app's features and content are identical in every region.
The interface is localized in Simplified Chinese and English and follows the
device language. The only region-dependent element is the price displayed for
the in-app purchase, which is localized automatically by the App Store.

6) REGULATED INDUSTRY AND THIRD-PARTY PROTECTED MATERIAL
The app is not in a regulated industry and does not provide any regulated
service. It does not display or link to third-party content: all illustrations,
animations, audio and text were created for this app by the developer, and no
third-party trademarks, brands or copyrighted works are included. There is
nothing to license or authorize.

7) IN-APP PURCHASE OVERVIEW
There is exactly one product: a non-consumable, "Tiny Sunflower Premium"
(product identifier com.sunflowertime.app.premium.lifetime), US$14.99 one-time,
not a subscription. It permanently unlocks:
  a. One-click care in the garden (watering, fertilizing and tending all plants
     at once). Free users can still care for each plant individually.
  b. Garden capacity from 4 pots to 12 pots.
  c. Unlimited growth-task templates (free: up to 5).
  d. Unlimited reward templates (free: up to 5).
  e. Up to 3 child profiles (free: 1).
  f. Title perks: plants gain small bonuses as the child's focus level rises.
Navigation to the purchase flow:
  Child screen -> "Parent Hub" (top-right) -> set or enter the parent PIN ->
  "My" tab -> gold card "Unlock full features" -> the premium page ->
  "Unlock now" (price is loaded from the App Store) or "Restore purchases".
The purchase entry is intentionally placed inside the PIN-protected parent
area, so a child cannot reach or trigger it.

ADDITIONAL NOTES

- The app does not require login to use any feature.
