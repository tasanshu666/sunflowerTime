2) PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Tiny Sunflower is a focus timer for children roughly 4-12 years old, used
together with a parent. The problem it solves: children's study time is easily
broken by distractions, and parents lack a simple, non-punitive way to build
focus habits and to see whether the time was actually spent. The value: the
child starts a timer, focused minutes earn an in-app currency ("sunlight"),
and the sunlight is spent growing plants in a small garden - so effort turns
into visible, patient progress instead of a score. The parent side lets the
parent define tasks and rewards, approve what the child claims, review daily/
weekly reports, and set eye-care breaks. Everything runs fully offline: no
accounts, no ads, no tracking, no data leaves the device.

3) HOW TO SET UP AND ACCESS THE MAIN FEATURES
No account, login or credentials are required - the app works immediately
after install.
- Child area: opens on launch. Five bottom tabs, in this order: Today, Growth,
  Garden, Store, Mine. Tap a task card on Today or Growth to start a focus
  session; the sun icon in the header shows the current sunlight balance.
- Parent area: tap the icon button in the TOP-RIGHT corner of the child screen
  (its accessibility label is "Parent Hub"). On the FIRST entry the app asks
  you to SET your own 4-digit PIN - there is no preset PIN, so please choose
  one, for example 1234. This gate exists so a child cannot reach the parent
  area or the purchase entry.
- Inside the parent area there are five tabs: Today, Rewards, Growth, Records
  and Settings. Today shows what the child has done; Rewards and Growth hold
  the reward and task templates; Records holds the daily and weekly focus
  reports; Settings holds the eye-care break, PIN change, language, links to
  the support website and privacy policy, the purchase entry, and "delete all
  data".
- Claim approval: when the child claims a task or a reward, it appears in the
  parent area for the parent to approve or reject.

4) EXTERNAL SERVICES, TOOLS AND PLATFORMS
None are used to deliver core functionality. All data (tasks, rewards, focus
history, garden state) is stored in a local database on the device; there is no
server, no cloud sync, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no AI service.
The only external system involved is Apple's In-App Purchase (StoreKit), used
solely to process the one-time purchase. The app uses a few open-source Flutter
plugins for local audio playback and for opening the system browser / mail app
(the support link and the support e-mail address); neither transmits app data.

5) REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
There are none. The app's features and content are identical in every region.
The interface is localized in Simplified Chinese and English and follows the
device language. The only region-dependent element is the price displayed for
the in-app purchase, which is localized automatically by the App Store.

7) IN-APP PURCHASE OVERVIEW
There is exactly one product: a non-consumable, "Tiny Sunflower Premium"
(product identifier com.sunflowertime.app.premium.lifetime), US$14.99 one-time,
not a subscription and with no auto-renewal. It permanently unlocks:
  a. One-click care in the garden (watering / fertilizing / tending all plants
     at once). Free users can still care for each plant individually.
  b. Garden capacity from 4 pots to 12 pots.
  c. Unlimited growth-task templates (free: up to 5).
  d. Unlimited reward templates (free: up to 5).
  e. Up to 3 child profiles (free: 1).
  f. Title perks: plants gain small bonuses as the child's focus level rises.

On the premium page these six benefits are shown as three summary cards -
"A roomier garden" (b + a), "Flexible routines" (c + d) and "Growth perks"
(f + e) - together with a "One-time · forever" badge and four short notes
(no subscription, how to restore on a new device, iOS/Android purchases are
not interchangeable, and that payment is handled by the App Store).

Navigation to the purchase flow:
  Child screen -> the icon button in the top-right corner ("Parent Hub") ->
  set/enter parent PIN -> "Settings" tab -> the "Unlock All" section ->
  the premium page -> "Unlock now · <price>" (the price is loaded from the
  App Store) or "Restore purchases".
The purchase entry is intentionally placed inside the PIN-protected parent
area, so a child cannot reach or trigger it.
