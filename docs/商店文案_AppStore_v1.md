**中文版（简中 · 2026-11-23 已与英文版对齐卖点）：**

```
小小葵番茄钟是一款专为孩子设计的专注计时 App。放下手机、选好任务开始专注，每一分钟都会变成「阳光」，用来种一座属于自己的花园。

【专注变成看得见的成长】
• 选一个任务开始计时，专注越久，阳光越多
• 用阳光种下植物，看它们从种子发芽、长大、开花；途中还能发现新物种、开花动画与轻柔背景音
• 全新天气系统让花园跟着阴晴变化，照料更有意思
• 花园守卫：用专注攒下的能量释放大招，守护你的植物
• 许愿树：完成成长任务解锁心愿，家长确认后即可实现
• 专注等级与称号：每一分钟都算数，累计解锁 40 个等级和 9 个里程碑称号，今日页免费可见
• 每日小目标，帮孩子一点点养成好习惯

【为孩子设计，由家长掌控】
• 孩子端只有计时、花园和小目标——无信息流、无广告、无陌生人
• 家长中心在右上角，需 PIN 进入；首次进入由您自行设置 PIN（至少 4 位），没有预设密码
• 在家长端布置任务、审批奖励、查看专注报告（日/周/月/年：累计专注分钟、有效专注日、趋势）；孩子端可查看阳光来源记录
• 防沉迷：娱乐内容每日达限后锁定，专注与成长始终开放

【阳光商店 · 现实奖励】
• 孩子可用攒下的阳光兑换现实奖励：一份小零食、多玩 10 分钟、看一集喜欢的动画片、一个小玩具、周末出游
• 孩子自助提交申请，但需家长确认完成后才扣阳光；在此之前阳光仍留在花园里

【轻柔护眼休息】
• 家长可开启护眼提醒并自定义间隔：全屏动画配中英文语音，陪孩子短暂休息，保护眼睛
• 护眼期间专注暂停，完整走完奖励少量阳光；家长可设间隔、可关闭「允许跳过」
• 夜间锁定：晚上 9 点后不能开始新一轮专注，向日葵要睡觉啦；每完成两场专注先休息 10 分钟，锁屏也照常计时

【隐私优先】
• 所有数据只保存在这台设备上，无需注册账号，无云同步、无追踪、无第三方分析
• 不收集、不上传、不出售任何数据

【一次性买断】
• 核心体验免费；可选的一次性购买解锁完整内容——不是订阅，付款后立即生效，可在您的其他设备恢复。
```

**英文版（US · 2026-11-23 三次修订·扩写定稿，2653 字符 / 上限 4000，直接复制进 ASC）：**

```
Tiny Sunflower is a calm focus timer that turns a child's focused minutes into a growing garden.

Put the phone down, pick a task, and focus. Every minute you stay focused earns sunlight. Spend sunlight on plants, watch them grow from seed to bloom, and keep the garden alive with water and care. Along the way, you'll discover new plants, gentle blooming animations, and soft background sounds.

BUILT FOR KIDS, CONTROLLED BY PARENTS
• Child side: a simple timer, a garden, and small daily goals — no feeds, no ads, no strangers.
• Parent side: protected by a PIN you set yourself. Approve rewards, set tasks, and review focus reports there.
• Anti-addiction: entertainment screens are locked after a daily limit while focus and growth stay open.
• Focus reports: parents can review daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly summaries — total focused minutes, active days, and trends — while kids can see a record of where their sunlight came from.

REWARDS IN REAL LIFE
• The Sunlight Store lets kids spend the sunlight they've earned on real-life rewards: a snack, ten more minutes of play, an episode of a favorite show, a small toy, or a weekend outing.
• Kids submit a request on their own, but nothing is deducted until a parent approves and marks it done. Until then, the sunlight stays in their garden.

GENTLE EYE-CARE BREAKS
• Parents can turn on gentle eye-care breaks to protect young eyes and choose how often they appear. A full-screen animation with soft narration, in English and Chinese, invites a short rest.
• Focusing pauses during the break, and seeing it through earns a little sunlight. Parents set the interval and decide whether a break can be skipped.
• Nighttime lock: after 9 PM, a new session won't start and the sunflower goes to sleep. After every two sessions, a ten-minute rest comes first — even if the screen is off.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Everything stays on this device. No account, no cloud, no tracking, no third-party analytics.
• We do not collect, upload, or sell any data.

IN THE GARDEN
• Weather: the garden changes with the sky, from bright sun to cloudy days.
• Garden Guard: use the energy you've earned from focusing on special moves to protect your plants.
• Wish Tree: complete growing goals to unlock wishes, then let a parent make them come true.
• Levels & titles: every focused minute counts toward levels (up to 40) and nine milestone titles, free to see on today's page with a progress badge.

ONE-TIME PURCHASE
• The core experience is free. An optional one-time purchase unlocks the full set of features — not a subscription, and purchases take effect right away. Restore anytime on your other devices.
```
