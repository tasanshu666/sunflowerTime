2) PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
A focus timer for children 4-12, used with a parent. It solves: study time is easily broken by distractions, and parents lack a simple, non-punitive way to build focus habits and verify the time was really spent. Focused minutes earn "sunlight", which grows plants in a small garden - effort becomes visible progress, not a score. Parents define tasks and rewards, approve claims, read daily/weekly reports and set eye-care breaks. Fully offline: no accounts, ads or tracking; no data leaves the device.

3) HOW TO SET UP AND ACCESS THE MAIN FEATURES
No account or login required.
- Child area opens on launch. Tabs: Today, Growth, Garden, Store, Mine. Tap a task card on Today or Growth to start a session; the sun icon shows the sunlight balance.
- Parent area: tap the icon in the TOP-RIGHT corner (accessibility label "Parent Hub"). On FIRST entry you SET your own 4-digit PIN - no preset PIN exists, so choose one, e.g. 1234. This keeps the child out of the parent area and the purchase entry.
- Parent tabs: Today, Rewards, Growth, Records, Settings (eye-care break, PIN change, language, support site, privacy policy, purchase entry, delete all data). The child's claims are approved or rejected here.

4) EXTERNAL SERVICES
None for core functionality. All data is in a local database on the device: no server, no cloud sync, no analytics, no ads, no AI. Only Apple In-App Purchase (StoreKit) processes the one-time purchase. Open-source Flutter plugins handle local audio playback and opening the browser/mail app; neither sends app data.

5) REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. Features and content are identical everywhere; the UI is localized in Simplified Chinese and English and follows the device language. Only the IAP price is localized by the App Store.

6) REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not a regulated industry, no regulated service. No third-party content: all illustrations, animations, audio and text were made for this app by the developer; no third-party trademarks or copyrighted works included.

7) IN-APP PURCHASE OVERVIEW
One product: non-consumable "Tiny Sunflower Premium" (com.sunflowertime.app.premium.lifetime), US$14.99 one-time, not a subscription, no auto-renewal. Unlocks permanently:
 a. One-click garden care (free: per plant only).
 b. Garden capacity 4 -> 12 pots.
 c. Unlimited growth-task templates (free: 5).
 d. Unlimited reward templates (free: 5).
 e. Up to 3 child profiles (free: 1).
 f. Title perks as the focus level rises.
Path: child screen -> top-right icon ("Parent Hub") -> PIN -> Settings tab -> "Unlock All" -> premium page -> "Unlock now · <price>" (price from the App Store) or "Restore purchases". The entry is inside the PIN-protected parent area, so a child cannot reach it.
